excerpt: 'I investigate the sources of pervasive factor model misspecification. Using zero-beta portfolios as diagnostic tools, I trace where risk pricing breaks down across model specifications, asset groups, and market conditions. The analysis distinguishes whether these failures arise from missing risk factors, model instability, non-linear pricing, or limits to arbitrage. By uncovering its economic origins, the paper seeks to explain why state-of-the-art factor models—despite their sophistication—continue to leave large and systematic pricing errors unexplained.'
